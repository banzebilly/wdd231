Who we are

A united voice for business in Gauteng

Founded in 1965, the Johannesburg Chamber of Commerce represents employers, entrepreneurs and non-profit organisations across the City of Johannesburg. We speak for our members in municipal and provincial policy forums, open doors to procurement and trade, and invest in the skills our city needs.

From township enterprises in Soweto to listed companies in Sandton, membership puts your business inside a working network of buyers, suppliers, mentors and advocates.
